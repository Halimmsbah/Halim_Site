Overview

What it is

G.UNIT is a full e-commerce system designed and developed end to end — from the customer-facing storefront through to the back office that keeps it running. The storefront covers the homepage, shop, product pages, cart, and a mobile-first responsive experience; the admin dashboard covers the full store cycle, from catalogue and orders to inventory, marketing, and staff permissions.

Highlights

Key features

  • Complete storefront: homepage, shop with filters and sorting, product pages, cart drawer, and checkout.
  • Variant system for colour and size, with a stock matrix that feeds straight into inventory.
  • Order lifecycle from placement to delivery — status timeline, invoices, courier assignment, and tracking.
  • Cash on delivery, Instapay transfer with proof upload, card and installments — each toggled per store.
  • Bilingual English/Arabic content: every product, page and setting is authored in both languages.
  • Per-governorate shipping zones, rates and delivery windows across Egypt.
  • Discounts and coupons: percentage, fixed, and free-delivery rules with usage limits and scheduling.
  • Content management for the homepage, collections, and lookbook — editable without touching code.
  • Role-based permissions across five roles, including masked customer data for support staff.
  • Responsive mobile experience with bottom navigation and a full-screen filter sheet.
