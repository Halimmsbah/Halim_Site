Overview

What it is

BEED Studios is a Riyadh film and content production house founded in 2023, working across writing and production. I designed and built their official website: a content-driven site that presents the studio, its services, its work and its partners, and runs fully in both Arabic and English — not a translated copy, but the same site with the entire layout mirrored between right-to-left and left-to-right.

Highlights

Key features

  • Bilingual Arabic and English, routed per locale so each language has its own URL.
  • Full RTL and LTR mirroring — layout, type and navigation all flip, not just the text.
  • Services section presenting the studio’s writing and production offering.
  • Work showcase for the studio’s films and projects.
  • Partners section for the clients and companies the studio works with.
  • Content-driven architecture with the content wired to the backend rather than hard-coded.
  • Bilingual admin dashboard to manage the hero video, home page sections, projects, partners and users.
  • Responsive across desktop, tablet and mobile.
  • A visual experience built to match the studio’s creative identity.
